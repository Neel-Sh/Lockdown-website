Common questions

Lockdown says it needs Screen Time access

Lockdown can only close apps after you allow Screen Time access. Open Lockdown and tap Allow when asked, or go to Settings › Screen Time and make sure Screen Time is on. If you previously declined, delete and reinstall Lockdown to see the prompt again.

A blocked app still opens

Check that the app is included in Choose what to close on the Ready screen. Apps added after a session started are blocked from the next session. If Screen Time access was turned off during a session, the session ends and apps unlock; turn it back on and lock in again.

How do I end a session early?

Tap End early on the session screen and confirm. In a Strict session the button is hidden; you can still end it at any time by turning off Lockdown in Settings › Screen Time.

Why can't I pick a session shorter than 15 minutes?

iOS requires scheduled blocks to last at least 15 minutes, so sessions range from 15 minutes to 4 hours.

The Live Activity isn't showing

Make sure Live Activity is on in Lockdown's Settings, and that Live Activities are allowed for Lockdown in Settings › Lockdown. Live Activities appear on the Lock Screen and, on supported iPhones, in the Dynamic Island.

I didn't get a notification when my session ended

Allow notifications for Lockdown in Settings › Notifications › Lockdown, and turn on Notify when time's up in Lockdown's Settings. Focus modes can also silence notifications.

How do I delete my history?

Open Lockdown, tap the gear icon, and choose Delete History. This removes every recorded session from your iPhone and can't be undone.

Does Lockdown work on iPad or Mac?

Lockdown is designed for iPhone and requires iOS 26 or later.
