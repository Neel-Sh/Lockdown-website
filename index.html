How it works

FAQ

Questions, answered.

How does Lockdown block apps on iPhone?

Lockdown uses Apple's Screen Time frameworks (Family Controls, Managed Settings and Device Activity). When you lock in, the apps you picked show a Lockdown screen instead of opening. At the end time, iOS lifts the block on its own, even if Lockdown isn't running.

Can Lockdown see which apps I use?

No. Apple only gives Lockdown opaque tokens for the apps you choose. Lockdown can show their names and icons on your screen but never learns which apps they are. Your session history stays on your iPhone.

How long can a study session be?

Pick 25, 45 or 60 minutes, or set a custom length anywhere from 15 minutes to 4 hours. 15 minutes is the shortest interval iOS allows for a scheduled block.

What is a Strict session?

A Strict session hides the End button so you're not tempted to quit. It's a promise to yourself, not a lock: turning off Lockdown in Settings › Screen Time always ends a session.

What happens if I open a blocked app?

You'll see a calm reminder, for example "You set this time aside for Biology. Instagram is available at 4:45 PM.", with buttons to close it or open Lockdown.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign-up, no account and no analytics. Everything Lockdown stores lives on your iPhone.

Which iPhones does Lockdown support?

Lockdown runs on iPhone with iOS 26 or later.
